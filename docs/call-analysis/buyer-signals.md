# Buyer Intent Signals & Evidence Matrix

Evidence-based intent signals observed across 18 real human sales calls.

| Intent Signal | Observed Transcript Evidence | Context & Interpretation | Recommended Vaani Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **HIGH_INTEREST** | *"Jaipur me Loha Mandi location bhej do, mai aakar by-face milunga aur machine dekhunga."* | Buyer is ready for physical inspection & deal closing. | Send yard GPS pin & schedule in-person visit. |
| **PURCHASE_INTENT** | *"With GST chahiye maam mujhe... 2024/2025 model JS 215/225 machine dikhao."* | Immediate requirement with GST billing ready. | Match active inventory & send photos + price quote immediately. |
| **PRICE_CHECKING** | *"Price kya bata rahe ho? 25 Lakh to 2019 model ka bahut zyada hai."* | Price evaluation & negotiation attempt. | Justify value (low hours, genuine company check) or offer alternatives. |
| **REQUIREMENT_MISMATCH** | *"Hyundai me chahiye 2024 model... JS nahi chahiye."* | Specific brand preference not currently matched in stock. | Record requirement & flag for sourcing alert. |
| **CALLBACK_REQUESTED** | *"Abhi mai drive kar raha hoon / meeting me hoon, aap WhatsApp par details daal do."* | High potential buyer unable to converse long. | Send WhatsApp catalog & set callback follow-up task. |
| **NOT_INTERESTED** | *"Nahi maam, abhi koi machine nahi chahiye, aage zarurat hogi to batayenge."* | Cold/Inactive lead. | Update status to Inactive & schedule long-term nurture. |

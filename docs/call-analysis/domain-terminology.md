# Domain Terminology Reference — Used Excavator Marketplace

Extracted strictly from 18 real human sales call recordings (Om Shyam Earthmovers).

| Term | Category | Meaning & Marketplace Context | Example Usage in Recordings | User Role | Confidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Poclain / Poklane** | Machine Category | Generic term used in Hindi/Hinglish market to refer to heavy hydraulic excavators. | *'Madam mujhe Pocala/Poclain machine chahiye thi...'* | Buyer / Seller | High (1.0) |
| **JS / JCB JS** | Brand & Series | JCB Tracked Excavator series (JS215, JS225). | *'JS me 215 hai, 225 hai, 2024 model 2025 model.'* | Both | High (1.0) |
| **Running Hours / Hours** | Machine Metric | Cumulative engine operating hours on hour-meter. | *'Sat aath hazar ghante chali hui hai.'* | Both | High (1.0) |
| **Model / Model Year** | Year of Manufacture | Year machine was produced/registered (e.g., 2024 model). | *'Tata ka 24 model hai... 33 Lakh price.'* | Both | High (1.0) |
| **Meter kharab** | Machine Condition | Broken or tampered hour meter; estimated hours used instead. | *'Meter kharab ho gaya hai, approx 9000 hours.'* | Seller | High (1.0) |
| **Mitti me chali / Pahado me chali / Ghat par chali** | Machine Application | Terrain/work type: Soil earthwork (light wear) vs Quarry/Mountains (heavy wear). | *'Hum to mitti me chali hui machine lete hai... Nadi ghaat par chali machine.'* | Both | High (1.0) |
| **GST / With GST** | Financial / Billing | GST tax invoice included with machine sale. | *'With GST chahiye maam mujhe... GST mil jayegi.'* | Buyer | High (1.0) |
| **Company check / Genuine machine** | Machine Verification | Maintenance verified via official OEM service center logs. | *'Company me check karoge to genuine machine milegi.'* | Both | High (1.0) |
| **Party** | Trade Role | External buyer or seller in broker/dealer network. | *'Ek party ke paas machine hai...'* | Both | High (1.0) |
| **Margin / Commission** | Business Model | Dealer profit markup vs brokerage commission. | *'Commission ka kaam nahi hai, firm me purchase karke margin par sell karte hai.'* | Salesperson | High (1.0) |
| **Loha Mandi (Jaipur)** | Location Yard | Heavy equipment machinery market hub in Jaipur, Rajasthan. | *'Jaipur me colony Loha Mandi me location rahega.'* | Salesperson | High (1.0) |
| **Sale Out / Available** | Availability Status | Inventory current availability status. | *'Do PC hai sale ke liye... Sale out ho gaya.'* | Both | High (1.0) |
| **Callback / WhatsApp Details** | Action Item | Standard follow-up via sending photos, specs & location on WhatsApp. | *'Aap photo aur details send kar do WhatsApp par.'* | Both | High (1.0) |

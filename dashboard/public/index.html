Questions

What you need to know before you start.

How do I sign in for the first time?

Open the sign-in page and choose Create account. Use a work email and a password of at least 12 characters. The account is created locally and you land in the CRM.

Why does the dashboard show demo data?

Local runs default to the grounded demo fixture so you can test the workflow without a database. Use the Real DB button in the CRM header when your Postgres connection is configured.

Where does the data live?

Local accounts and sessions live in the dashboard JSON store. Sales data can come from the demo fixture or from Postgres when VAANI_DATABASE_URL is set.

What is the difference between the landing page and the CRM?

The landing page explains the product. The CRM at /app.html is the signed-in workspace where your team works leads, inventory, and follow-ups.
